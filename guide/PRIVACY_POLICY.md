# Privacy Policy for Case Changer

Effective date: 3 September 2026

Case Changer is a Google Docs add-on made by Ikhwan Ariff ("we", "us"). It changes the case of text you select in a Google Docs document, for example to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case.

## What the add-on can access

When you install Case Changer, Google asks you to grant two permissions:

- **View and manage documents that this application has been installed in** (`documents.currentonly`). This lets the add-on read the text you select and write it back in the new case. It cannot open any other document in your Google Drive.
- **Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications** (`script.container.ui`). This lets the add-on show its menu and sidebar.

## What we collect

Nothing. Case Changer does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information or document content.

- The text you select is processed inside Google's Apps Script platform and written straight back into your document. It is never sent to us or to any third party.
- We keep no copies of your documents or text.
- We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- We do not see your name, email address or Google account details.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Case Changer's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing the add-on

You can uninstall Case Changer at any time from **Extensions > Add-ons > Manage add-ons** inside Google Docs, or revoke its access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Because we store nothing, there is no data for us to delete.
