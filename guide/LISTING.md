## Application name (max 50 characters)

Case Changer

## Short description (max 200 characters)

Change selected text in Google Docs™, Sheets™ and Slides™ to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Sentence case, Title Case and more, keeping bold, links and colours.

## Detailed description (max 16,000 characters)

Case Changer fixes the case of any text in Google Docs™, Google Sheets™ and Google Slides™ in one click.

Highlight the text, then pick a style from the Extensions menu or the sidebar:

• UPPERCASE
• lowercase
• Sentence case (first letter of each sentence, and "i" becomes "I")
• Title Case (smart: short words like "of", "and" and "the" stay lowercase unless they start or end the title)
• Capitalize Each Word
• iNVERSE cASE
• aLtErNaTiNg cAsE

What makes it different:

• Formatting is kept. Bold, italic, underline, colours, fonts, highlights and links stay exactly where they were.
• Works on any selection: a word, a sentence, several paragraphs, list items or whole tables.
• Fast. Change a paragraph and keep typing.
• Private. Nothing leaves your document. The add-on only asks for access to the document it is open in, and stores nothing.
• Keeps acronyms such as NASA or KL when you switch to Sentence case or Title Case.
• Title Case knows which small words to keep lowercase in English, Bahasa Malaysia and Bahasa Indonesia, and you can add your own.
• Nothing selected? Change the whole document, sheet or presentation in one go.
• Free, with no account, sign-up or ads.

Tip: made a mistake? Press Cmd+Z or Ctrl+Z to undo, just like any other edit.

Works in Google Docs™ (selected text), Google Sheets™ (selected cells; formulas, numbers and dates are never touched) and Google Slides™ (highlighted text, text boxes, shapes and tables).
