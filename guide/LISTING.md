## Detailed description (max 16,000 characters)

Case Changer fixes the case of any text in your document in one click.

Highlight the text, then pick a style from the Extensions menu or the sidebar:

• UPPERCASE
• lowercase
• Sentence case (first letter of each sentence, and "i" becomes "I")
• Title Case (smart: short words like "of", "and" and "the" stay lowercase unless they start or end the title)
• Capitalize Each Word
• iNVERSE cASE
• aLtErNaTiNg cAsE

What makes it different:

• Formatting is kept. Bold, italic, underline, colours, fonts, highlights and links stay exactly where they were.
• Works on any selection: a word, a sentence, several paragraphs, list items or whole tables.
• Fast. Change a paragraph and keep typing.
• Private. Nothing leaves your document. The add-on only asks for access to the document it is open in, and stores nothing.
• Free, with no account, sign-up or ads.

Tip: made a mistake? Press Cmd+Z or Ctrl+Z to undo, just like any other edit.

Currently supports Google Docs.
